Last updated / Son yenilənmə: 2026-11-19

1. Who we are

"Azər baba və Fidan" ("the channel", "we") is an independent creator project that produces short animated educational videos in Azerbaijani. The application "fidanvebaba-publisher" is an internal tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

2. Data we access

The application signs in only with the channel owner's own accounts. Through the official APIs it accesses:

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API v3, Google OAuth): permission to upload videos and captions to the channel owner's YouTube channel (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl). We do not read, collect or store any other Google account data, viewer data or analytics.
  • TikTok (Content Posting API): basic profile info of the channel owner's account and permission to publish videos to that account.
  • Instagram / Facebook (Instagram Graph API): the channel's Instagram professional account ID and permission to publish Reels to it.

We do not collect any personal data from viewers or from any other user. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

3. How data is used, stored and shared

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens are used solely to publish the channel's own videos. They are stored encrypted (as encrypted secrets in the channel's private infrastructure) and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties.
  • Uploaded videos and their metadata (title, description, tags, captions) are created by the channel and become public on the respective platforms by the channel owner's choice.
  • Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and are deleted when the integration is removed.

4. Third-party services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Meta (Instagram) services are governed by their own policies: TikTok Privacy Policy, Instagram Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking access / deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok — Settings › Security › Manage app permissions; Meta — Settings › Apps and websites. After revocation the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
